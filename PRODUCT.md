# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS

## Users

Content creators, YouTube channels, and brands reviewing the thumbnail and social media design portfolio to evaluate quality and hire for design projects.

## Product Purpose

Serves as a professional portfolio, landing page, and marketing site to showcase design work, demonstrate quality, and secure new client projects.

## Positioning

A dedicated, high-impact showcase specifically tailored for thumbnail and social media design that prioritizes visual proof of skill.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients (creators and brands) will view the site on desktop and mobile devices during candidate evaluation or vendor sourcing, expecting immediate visual impact and a seamless way to make contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Must support high-quality image galleries or grids for thumbnails and social media assets. Must operate as a static site without a backend. 

## Product Principles

- **Visuals First**: The interface should recede to let the portfolio assets take center stage.
- **Frictionless Contact**: Clear and immediate paths for a prospective client to reach out.
- **Fast and Responsive**: Image-heavy content must load quickly and perform well across all device classes.

## Evidence on Hand

The project directory currently contains an empty `assets` folder; portfolio images and copy will need to be provided or generated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web accessibility practices, ensuring proper alt-text for all portfolio imagery and sufficient contrast for any text elements.
